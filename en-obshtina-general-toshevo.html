Where to live in General-Toshevo Municipality?

General-Toshevo Municipality (Dobrich Province) has 42 places. The average score is 4.0 out of 10. 8 of them have a GP on site and 4 have a school. The highest is General Toshevo with 8.3 (very good). About 14,850 people are registered at a permanent address. Unemployment in the municipality is about 11% (a rough guide from people registered at labour offices, 2025).

How the score is calculated

For every place we look at how easily you can reach a school, a GP, a pharmacy, a dentist and a hospital, and how far it is from the provincial capital. If the service is in the place itself it gets the full share. If not, the share falls with the distance to the nearest place that has it. Distances are in a straight line. The full method and the settings are on the site.
