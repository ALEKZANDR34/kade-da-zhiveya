Where to live in Targovishte Municipality?

Targovishte Municipality (Targovishte Province) has 52 places. The average score is 5.1 out of 10. 17 of them have a GP on site and 8 have a school. The highest is Targovishte with 10.0 (excellent). About 70,308 people are registered at a permanent address. Unemployment in the municipality is about 7% (a rough guide from people registered at labour offices, 2025).

How the score is calculated

For every place we look at how easily you can reach a school, a GP, a pharmacy, a dentist and a hospital, and how far it is from the provincial capital. If the service is in the place itself it gets the full share. If not, the share falls with the distance to the nearest place that has it. Distances are in a straight line. The full method and the settings are on the site.
