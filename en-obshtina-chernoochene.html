Where to live in Chernoochene Municipality?

Chernoochene Municipality (Kardzhali Province) has 51 places. The average score is 5.0 out of 10. 4 of them have a GP on site and 4 have a school. The highest is Chernoochene with 8.7 (excellent). About 16,261 people are registered at a permanent address (no data for some of the places). Unemployment in the municipality is about 17% (a rough guide from people registered at labour offices, 2025).

How the score is calculated

For every place we look at how easily you can reach a school, a GP, a pharmacy, a dentist and a hospital, and how far it is from the provincial capital. If the service is in the place itself it gets the full share. If not, the share falls with the distance to the nearest place that has it. Distances are in a straight line. The full method and the settings are on the site.
